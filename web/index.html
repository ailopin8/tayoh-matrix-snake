    03 / Resource ecology
    Food renews into a shared pool. Both snakes rank reachable resources by benefit, distance, escape space and competition. Each observes the other’s body and predicts its next step. Harmful or wasteful resources are avoided. Manual steering lets you test their effects.
    Open-ended means renewable inputs and bounded structural adaptation; survival is never guaranteed. Each body is capped at 24 segments and excess growth returns to compost.
    

    04 / Boundary & compression laboratory
    A straight threshold in a folded feature space pulls back to a square boundary. This explicit many-to-one mapping is an abstraction, not a physical paper-folding proof.
    F(x,y) = (max(|x−4.5|, |y−4.5|), 0). A cut u = r has preimage ∂[4.5−r,4.5+r]². A line under a general nonrigid fold does not necessarily produce a closed separator; topology and the cut mapping must be specified.
